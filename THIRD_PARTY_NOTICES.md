## Prismacolor Premier

150 pencils with their numbers, names and HEX values, from the
[Complete List of Prismacolor Premier Colored Pencils](https://www.jennyscrayoncollection.com/2020/04/complete-list-of-prismacolor-premier.html)
on Jenny's Crayon Collection. The page states no license. PC 935 Black is listed there with a malformed HEX value
(`0000000`), so its RGB value (0, 0, 0) is used instead.
